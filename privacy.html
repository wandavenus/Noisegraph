Privacy Policy — Noisegraph

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Noisegraph is a music player for Android built around your local library. This policy explains what data the app handles, and what happens if you choose to use the optional account and cloud backup feature.

Your music stays on your device

Your music files, library, listening history, and playback data stay on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Optional sign-in and cloud backup

Signing in is optional. If you open the Account page and choose "Sign in with Google", sign-in is handled by Google through our backend provider, Supabase. From Google we receive your email address and a unique user ID, used only to identify your account.

Nothing is uploaded until you tap "Backup". A backup contains:

  • Your playlists
  • Your favorite songs
  • App settings
  • Equalizer state

Backups are stored with Supabase (Singapore region) under your account, so you can restore them on the same or a new device. A backup never includes your music files.

What we do not do

  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • We never sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing

Keeping and deleting your data

Your backup is kept until you ask us to delete it. Signing out stops further backups. To delete your account and its backup data, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
