02 / What it's about

Less noise. More listening.

Built for people who keep their music close and care about how one song gives way to the next.

[01] / LIBRARY

Your local music, organized

Browse songs, albums, artists, and playlists from your device. Search and library controls keep your own collection at the center.

LOCAL FILESSEARCHPLAYLISTS
[02] / NOW PLAYING

A player that feels alive

Choose the animated Fluid background or artwork blur, with expressive cover art and motion designed around the music.

FLUIDARTWORK BLUR
[03] / LYRICS

Lyrics in sync with the song

Open the lyrics overlay for playback-synced lines and karaoke-style highlighting. Lyrics can come from embedded, local, and supported online sources.

SYNCED LYRICSKARAOKE
[04] / EQUALIZER

Shape the sound your way

Adjust the equalizer with presets or custom bands, then fine-tune playback with crossfeed, acoustic enhancement, stereo widening, and reverb.

EQ PRESETSAUDIO EFFECTS
[05] / LOUDNESS

More consistent track levels

ReplayGain-aware playback helps reduce perceived volume jumps between tracks when usable loudness metadata is available.

REPLAYGAINLOUDNESS
[06] / SPEED & PITCH

Change pace, shape pitch

Native Signalsmith Stretch processing supports playback speed and pitch changes, with sound quality depending on the amount of adjustment.

TIME STRETCHPITCH SHIFT
[07] / TRANSITIONS

Make track handoffs smoother

Set crossfade behavior and duration for transitions between songs. AutoMix adds a separate, analysis-driven way to plan the next track.

CROSSFADEAUTOMIX
[08] / PLAYBACK

Queue it up. Wind down.

Manage what plays next and use the sleep timer to end a listening session without having to stop playback manually.

PLAY QUEUESLEEP TIMER
[09] / PURE PLAYBACK

Pure Playback

Pure Playback provides an app-wide processing bypass for a more direct playback path. Sound-processing controls are locked while it is active.

04 / Good to know

Before you press play.

A few details about the project and current builds.

What is Noisegraph?

Noisegraph is an Android music player project focused on local playback, a modern Now Playing experience, and playback transitions.

How do I download it?

Open the latest GitHub Release, download the APK asset, and install it on your Android device. Read the release notes before installing.

Which devices are supported?

The published build targets Android on ARM64 (arm64-v8a). Compatibility can vary by release, so check the notes for the build you download.

Is Noisegraph still in development?

Yes. Features may change between builds. Follow the repository for updates and use GitHub Issues to report bugs or suggest improvements.

Where can I report a problem?

Open a GitHub Issue with reproduction steps, your Android version, device model, and relevant logs or screenshots.
